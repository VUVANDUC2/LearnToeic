Single Passage: Memorandum (Questions 147–148)

MEMORANDUM

Please note that the annual performance review period will **commence on November 1st** and conclude on **November 30th**...

When will the performance review period begin?
  • A) On November 30th
  • C) On November 1st
  • D) After the self-assessment form is submitted
Correct Answer & Explanation

**Answer:** C.
**Explanation:** The memo explicitly states, "The annual performance review period will **commence on November 1st**."
